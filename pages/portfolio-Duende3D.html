Duende 3D Artbook

Duende 3D Artbook is a children’s book I created and self-published, blending painting, storytelling, and immersive technology. The story follows Blu, an alien child who leaves their planet in search of true magic. After crash-landing on the island of Duende, Blu discovers that the island has been waiting for them, marking the beginning of a journey of self-discovery and wonder. I painted the full collection of artworks that bring Blu’s story to life, then wrote the accompanying narrative. To push the project further, I incorporated Chromadepth technology, transforming the entire book into a 3D experience. This innovation made Duende one of the first children’s art books of its kind.
