Kitchen SOP Platform

I architected and built a full-stack multilingual SOP platform for Risbo BK from the ground up, starting with a Notion prototype before engineering an internal web app using React, Node.js, and SQL. Built for a team of 20 employees, the system features role-based access, an employee progress dashboard, and a secure admin panel that tracks checklist completion and training module progress. An OpenAI-powered chatbot delivers real-time staff guidance on demand. Supporting English, French, and Spanish, the platform was designed with expansion in mind, giving ownership the infrastructure to replicate operations and onboard new locations with consistency and speed.
