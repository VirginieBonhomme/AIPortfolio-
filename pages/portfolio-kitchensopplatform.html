Kitchen SOP Platform

I built a multilingual SOP platform for Risbo BK to streamline kitchen operations, improve training, and eliminate communication gaps in a fast-paced environment. Designed as a centralized hub, the tool provides easy access to role-specific procedures, step-by-step instructions, and daily checklists, allowing staff to work efficiently without confusion or constant supervision. With support for English, French, and Spanish, it ensures all team members can clearly understand expectations, reducing errors and creating a more cohesive workflow. This system transforms scattered knowledge into a structured, scalable solution that enhances onboarding, consistency, and overall kitchen performance.
