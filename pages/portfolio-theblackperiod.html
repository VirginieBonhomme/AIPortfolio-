The Black Period Podcast

The Black Period Podcast is a conversation series I co-created with Sidra Greene, beginning in the summer of 2020. The series revisits the 1926 Crisis magazine symposium, “The Negro in Art – How Shall He Be Portrayed?” Nearly a century later, many of the same questions still confront Black artists. Across seven episodes, we engage in in-depth conversations with Black artists and curators who reflect on these enduring issues, exploring how representation, identity, and artistic freedom continue to shape the cultural landscape.
