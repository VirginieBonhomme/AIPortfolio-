Paris in Black and White

Paris in Black and White was created during my first trip to Paris in 2018, where I spent the summer visiting family and immersing myself in art history. Over the course of a month, I captured more than 200 black-and-white photographs that documented my experiences and encounters with the city. Paris, often called the city of love, revealed itself to me not just through its iconic beauty but through the profound connections I formed with its rhythm, history, and atmosphere. This series reflects that intimate dialogue between myself and the city.
