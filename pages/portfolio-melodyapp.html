MelodySketch

I designed MelodySketch, an iOS app for a songwriter who needed a faster, smarter alternative to Voice Memos. She was losing melody ideas in the seconds it took to open an app and had hundreds of unlabelled recordings she never revisited. I owned the full UX/UI across nine screens in Figma, designing a lock screen widget for instant capture, a lyric pad that runs alongside recordings, and a complete project and tagging system for organizing ideas. I delivered a full design system and prototype scoped to the MVP.
