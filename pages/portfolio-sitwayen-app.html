Sitwayen App

Sitwayen App is a bilingual learning web app built to help Haitian Creole and English speakers prepare for the U.S. naturalization civics exam. I created this app after volunteering with members of my Haitian community who were preparing for the test and realizing there weren’t enough accessible resources that truly met them where they were. Many needed support that was clear, culturally relevant, and confidence building. In response, I developed Sitwayen to provide a more supportive learning experience, combining practice tests, flashcards, audio support, state specific study content, and progress tracking in a mobile friendly interface. The goal is to help users feel prepared, confident, and empowered as they study for this important milestone.
