Hi, my name is Virginie

I'm Virginie Bonhomme, a Creative Technologist and artist based in New York City. I build systems, tools, and experiences that bring clarity to complex ideas and real-world operations. My work lives at the intersection of culture, technology, and design. As an artist, I paint and create culturally rooted projects like my Lakay series, which evolved into People of the Future, an AI-driven exhibition reimagining Haiti through innovation and design. As a technologist, I engineer practical tools including multilingual SOP platforms that streamline operations in fast-paced environments. With a background in Art History and software engineering, and ongoing studies in cybersecurity, I approach everything I build with both creativity and structure in mind. At the core of my work is a commitment to preserving heritage, shaping future narratives, and creating tools that are thoughtful, functional, and secure.
