Hi,

I’m Virginie Solomon-Bonhomme, a Creative Technologist and IT support professional based in New York City. I build practical and creative technology that helps people learn, work, and preserve culture more clearly.

My projects range from support-focused systems to cultural and creative tools. I’ve built Chord Machine CM-01, an interactive music composition tool with Web Audio and MIDI support, and Sitwayen App, a bilingual civics study platform for Haitian Creole and English speakers. I also create culturally rooted artwork and AI-driven projects, including People of the Future, an exhibition reimagining Haiti through innovation, memory, and design.

My background in Art History, software engineering, and cybersecurity shapes how I build. I care about clear systems, accessible design, strong documentation, and secure workflows. Whether I’m making an internal operations tool, a learning platform, or an art-driven digital experience, my goal is the same: to create technology that is useful, thoughtful, and grounded in real human needs.
